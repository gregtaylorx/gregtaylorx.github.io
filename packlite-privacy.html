Privacy Policy

PackLite  ·  HeadFirstServices LLC  ·  Last updated: August 2026

HeadFirstServices LLC ("we", "us", or "our") built PackLite — a smart, weather-aware packing intelligence app for travelers. This Privacy Policy explains what data PackLite collects, why, and how it is protected. It applies only to PackLite; our other apps (VisaStay, ZoneLink) have separate policies and separate databases — your data is not shared between apps.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account you provide your email address and a password (stored as a salted hash — we never see it in plain text). A unique user ID is generated and linked to all your data in PackLite.

Packing lists and items

The core data PackLite stores on your behalf:

  • Packing lists — destination(s), travel dates, trip purpose, transport type, bag type, laundry access, and climate summary
  • Packing items — item name, category, quantity, estimated weight, and checklist state (packed / not packed)
  • Post-trip feedback — after each trip you can optionally mark items as used, not used, or "wish I'd brought." This feedback is stored to power personalised future recommendations within your account only. It is never shared with other users.
  • Bag profiles — saved bag types with optional weight and volume limits you define
  • Templates — any custom packing templates you create

Weather data

PackLite fetches weather forecasts and historical climate data for your destination(s) via Open-Meteo. Only destination coordinates (latitude/longitude) are sent to Open-Meteo — no account identifier, email, or personal information is included in these requests. Weather results are cached locally on your device and, when a list is saved, stored against that list in your account.

Subscription status

Subscription purchases (Pro plan) are processed by RevenueCat and the underlying app store (Google Play or Apple App Store). We receive only a subscription status flag (active / expired / trial). Your payment details are never transmitted to or stored by us.

Biometric lock (optional)

If you enable biometric app lock (fingerprint / Face ID), authentication is handled entirely by your device's operating system. We never receive, transmit, or store any biometric data — PackLite only stores a local flag that biometric lock is enabled for your user ID.

Device and usage data

PackLite contains no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, no crash reporter, and no behavioural tracking library. We do not collect device model, OS version, IP address logs, or session recordings.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalise the core packing features of the app
  • To improve packing suggestions over time using your own past-trip feedback (within your account only — not pooled across users)
  • To send transactional emails you explicitly request (e.g. password reset)
  • To enforce subscription entitlements (Free vs. Pro)
  • To allow you to export or delete your data on request
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties. We do not send marketing emails.

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, all personal data associated with it is permanently deleted from our systems within 7 days, except where retention is required by applicable law. Anonymised, non-identifiable aggregates (e.g. total number of packing lists created) may be retained indefinitely.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Portability — export your data in machine-readable format (available in-app: Profile → Export My Data)
  • Restriction / objection — request that we limit or stop processing your data in certain circumstances
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent (e.g. email notifications), you can withdraw at any time in-app

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@headfirstservices.com. We will respond within 30 days. If you are in the European Economic Area, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

6. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account from within PackLite (Profile → Delete Account). This opens an email to privacy@headfirstservices.com; your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted within 7 days.

Deleting your PackLite account does not affect accounts you may have in our other apps (VisaStay, ZoneLink) — each app maintains a completely separate data scope.

7. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach likely to result in risk to your rights and freedoms, we will:

  • Notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware, where required by applicable law (including GDPR Article 33)
  • Notify affected users without undue delay where the breach poses a high risk to their rights and freedoms
  • Contact you by email at the address registered with your account

8. International Data Transfers

Your data is stored on servers in the United States (Supabase / AWS US East). If you are in the European Economic Area, this constitutes a transfer of personal data outside the EEA. Such transfers are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) incorporated into our agreement with Supabase.

9. Children's Privacy

PackLite is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact privacy@headfirstservices.com and we will delete it promptly.

10. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest, row-level security on all database tables (each user can only access their own data), and bcrypt password hashing. No method of internet transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable precautions to protect your information.

11. Changes to This Policy

When we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. For material changes, we will notify you by email or in-app notice at least 14 days before the change takes effect.
