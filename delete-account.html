VisaStay

  1. Open VisaStay and go to Profile
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm

Deletes your account and all associated data, including your profile (email, passport country, home currency), trips, and saved visas. Email requests are processed within 7 days.

ZoneLink

  1. Open ZoneLink and go to Profile
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm

Deletes your account and all associated data immediately, including saved Favorites and settings. Email requests are processed within 7 days. Want to remove only some data? See Delete ZoneLink data.

TripGear

  1. Open TripGear and go to Profile
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm

Deletes your account and all associated data, including packing lists, items, templates, bag profiles, and post-trip feedback. Email requests are processed within 7 days.
