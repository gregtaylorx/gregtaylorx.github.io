Delete Your CostSync Account

CostSync account deletion is instant and self-service — no email or waiting period required.

In the app:

  1. Open CostSync
  2. Go to Profile
  3. Tap Delete Account

Your account and all associated data — trips, expenses, receipts, budgets, and wallets — are permanently deleted immediately.

If you no longer have the app installed or can't sign in, email us instead:

privacy@headfirstservices.com

Please include your registered email address. We will process your deletion request within 7 days and confirm via email when complete.
