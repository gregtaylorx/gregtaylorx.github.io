Privacy Policy

HeadFirstServices LLC  ·  Last updated: November 2026

HeadFirstServices LLC ("we", "us", or "our") operates the following mobile applications (collectively, "the Apps"):

  • VisaStay — visa duration tracker for frequent international travelers
  • TripGear (formerly PackLite) — weather-aware packing lists for travelers
  • ZoneLink — time-zone planner for remote teams and travelers
  • CostSync — trip-based, multi-currency travel expense tracker
  • Any future apps published under the HeadFirstServices LLC or Outbound Suite brand

ChewPaca has its own privacy policy at chewpaca.headfirstservices.com/privacy and is not covered by this one.

This Privacy Policy applies to all of the above. Where practices differ between apps, that is noted explicitly. Each app uses its own separate database — your VisaStay, TripGear, ZoneLink, and CostSync accounts and data are not shared or linked between apps unless you explicitly connect them.

1. Information We Collect

Account information All apps

When you create an account you provide your email address and a password (stored as a salted hash — we never see it in plain text), or you sign in with Apple or Google, in which case we receive your email address from that provider. A unique user ID is generated and associated with all your data. Each app maintains its own separate account, even if you use the same email address across apps.

Profile information All apps

You may optionally provide a home currency VisaStay CostSync, passport / nationality VisaStay, or locale, tax country, and preferred expense categories CostSync. This is used to personalize the app experience and is never sold or shared with advertisers.

Trip and travel data VisaStay

Trip records you create include: destination country, city, start and end dates, passport used for entry, visa type or program, and optional free-text notes. You can also save visas you hold (visa type, country, and dates). This data is stored securely in your account and is not accessible to other users.

Additional alert recipients VisaStay

If you choose to send alert emails to someone else (for example a partner, accountant, or employer), we store the email address you enter and send that person only the alerts you set up. They are not signed up for anything else, and you can remove them at any time in the app.

Trip and expense data CostSync

CostSync organizes your data around Trips: name, countries, cities, dates, purpose, and default currency. Within a trip, expenses you log include: amount, currency, exchange rate, converted amount, category, merchant, date/time, optional notes, location, and payment method. If you use receipt scanning, the receipt image and any OCR-extracted fields (merchant, amount, date, tax) are stored in your account. Budgets and wallet balances you set are also stored. None of this is shared with other users unless you explicitly enable a shared trip and invite them.

Packing data TripGear

Packing lists you create include: destination(s), travel dates, trip purpose, transport type, bag type, and individual packing items with their status (packed / used / not used / wish I'd brought). Post-trip feedback you submit is stored to power personalized future recommendations — within your account only.

Time zone data ZoneLink

ZoneLink lets you save "Favorites" — named groups of time zones. Data you create includes: Favorite names and colors, and the entries in each Favorite (a label you choose — such as a person's name, team, or city — plus a time zone and optional work/sleep hours). If you use jet lag tracking, we store the time zones you traveled from and to, your arrival time, and your device's last time zone. ZoneLink never uses your location.

If you add another person to a Favorite (for example, a colleague's name and time zone), you are providing us with limited information about that person on your behalf. Only the label and time zone you choose to enter are stored — we do not independently verify or enrich this information, and it is visible only to your account.

Notification preferences VisaStay

If you enable push or email notifications, we store your configured alert rules (e.g. days before visa expiry) and your device push token. You can delete or disable these at any time from within the app.

Biometric lock (optional) VisaStay ZoneLink CostSync

If you enable biometric app lock, authentication is handled entirely by your device's operating system (Face ID / fingerprint). We never receive, transmit, or store any biometric data — the app only stores a local yes/no preference that biometric lock is enabled.

Device and usage data

We do not collect analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or behavioral tracking data. We do not use any third-party analytics SDK.

Payment data Pro subscriptions

Subscription purchases are processed by RevenueCat and the underlying app store (Apple App Store or Google Play). We receive only a subscription status flag (active / expired). Your payment card details are never transmitted to or stored by us.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalize the core features of the app you are using
  • To send transactional emails you explicitly request (e.g. password reset, visa expiry alerts, and the monthly digest you configured)
  • To enforce subscription entitlements (free vs. Pro)
  • To allow you to export or delete your data on request
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, all personal data associated with that account is permanently deleted from our systems within 7 days, except where retention is required by applicable law.

Anonymized, non-identifiable aggregates may be retained indefinitely.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Portability — export your data in machine-readable format (available in-app: Profile → Export My Data)
  • Restriction / objection — request that we limit or stop processing your data in certain circumstances
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent (e.g. email notifications), you can withdraw at any time in-app

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@headfirstservices.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the European Economic Area, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

6. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account directly within each app (Profile → Delete Account). In VisaStay, ZoneLink, and TripGear this is self-service — your account and its data are deleted from within the app, no email required. If you can't use the app, email privacy@headfirstservices.com and we will permanently delete your account and all associated data within 7 days.

ZoneLink You can also delete individual Favorites, or entries within them, without deleting your account. These are removed from the app immediately, but the underlying record is retained on our servers (not immediately purged) for sync reliability across your devices — it is never displayed or used once deleted. Deleting your full account remains the way to guarantee immediate, permanent removal of everything. See Delete ZoneLink data.

Deleting your account in one app does not affect accounts in our other apps — each app maintains a separate data scope.

You can also request deletion for one or more apps at once at privacy.headfirstservices.com/delete-account.html.

7. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach likely to result in risk to your rights and freedoms, we will:

  • Notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, where required by applicable law (including GDPR Article 33)
  • Notify affected users without undue delay where the breach poses a high risk to their rights and freedoms (GDPR Article 34)
  • Contact you by email at the address registered with your account
  • Describe the nature of the breach, data affected, likely consequences, and steps we are taking to address it

8. International Data Transfers

Your data is stored on servers in the United States (Supabase / AWS US East). If you are in the European Economic Area, this constitutes a transfer of personal data outside the EEA. Such transfers are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) incorporated into our agreement with Supabase.

9. Children's Privacy

The Apps are not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact privacy@headfirstservices.com and we will delete it promptly.

10. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest, row-level security on all database tables so users can only access their own data, and bcrypt password hashing. No method of internet transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable precautions to protect your information.

11. Changes to This Policy

When we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. For material changes, we will notify you by email or in-app notice at least 14 days before the change takes effect. Continued use of the Apps after that date constitutes acceptance.
