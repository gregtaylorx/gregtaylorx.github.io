Delete Your ZoneLink Data

ZoneLink by HeadFirstServices LLC. You can delete some or all of your data without deleting your account.

Boards, entities, meetings, and itinerary segments

Delete any board, entity, meeting slot, or itinerary segment directly in the app — open the item and choose Delete. It's removed from your view immediately and is no longer visible or usable anywhere in the app.

Retention: for sync reliability across your devices, the underlying record is retained on our servers rather than immediately purged. It is never displayed, returned to the app, or used for any purpose once deleted. We're working toward a fixed purge schedule for this retained data; until then, deleting your full account (see below) is the way to guarantee immediate, permanent removal.

Calendar Sync (Google Calendar import)

Go to Profile → Calendar Sync and turn the toggle off. This immediately and permanently deletes every event ZoneLink has imported from your Google Calendar — no retention period.

Everything (full account deletion)

To permanently delete your entire ZoneLink account and all associated data immediately, with no retention:

Delete My Account
