ChewPaca Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

ChewPaca collects no personal data. No account, no backend, nothing ever leaves your device.

Summary

ChewPaca is a mealtime coaching app that uses your device's camera to detect open-mouth chewing and reward closed-mouth chewing with an on-screen alpaca character. All camera processing happens locally, on the device, in real time. No video, image, or audio frame is ever uploaded, stored remotely, or seen by anyone — including us.

What we collect

Nothing. ChewPaca has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no backend server. There is no database that stores information about you or your child. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Camera and microphone

  • Camera: used only to detect jaw and lip movement during a meal, entirely on-device. Frames are analyzed in memory and immediately discarded. Nothing is written to disk or transmitted.
  • Microphone: off by default and opt-in only. If enabled, audio is analyzed on-device as a supporting signal and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted. A visible on-screen indicator shows when the microphone is active.

Data stored on your device

Meal progress, streaks, settings, and your alpaca collection are stored locally on your device only. This data is never synced to a server and never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently.

Purchases

ChewPaca offers a one-time unlock purchase, processed entirely through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Purchase validation is handled by RevenueCat, our payment infrastructure provider, which receives only the information necessary to confirm your purchase (such as an anonymous purchase receipt and device identifier for entitlement purposes). RevenueCat does not receive camera data, meal data, or any information about your child. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for details.

Children's privacy

ChewPaca is designed to be used by children under adult supervision, with a parent or guardian setting up and controlling the app. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children, and structurally cannot — there is no account system and no data transmission of any kind. A parental gate is required before camera access, purchases, or settings changes.

Third parties

We do not share, sell, or transmit any data to third parties, because we do not collect any. The only third-party service involved is RevenueCat, solely for purchase validation as described above.

Changes to this policy

If ChewPaca's data practices ever change (for example, an optional future cloud-sync feature), this page will be updated first and the change will be clearly described, along with an updated "Last updated" date.
